On September 23, 2026, the [H3](https://h3geo.org) community met at Uber's office in New York City for the first [H3 Conference](https://www.fused.io/h3-conference), hosted by [Fused](https://www.fused.io) and Uber. Eleven talks covered crop acreage, climate data, basketball, energy infrastructure, indoor positioning, newsroom maps and more.

Every recording is in the [H3 Conference 2026 playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLV-YgmSOYvwQ). Below is a short guide to each talk, with a link to its full write-up.

### The talks

- **[Spatial Intelligence Using Hexagons](/blog/spatial-intelligence-using-hexagons)** by Sina Kashuk and Isaac Brodsky (Fused). Eight years after presenting H3 at Uber around the time it was open-sourced, Sina rebuilds that talk with AI and scores the "vibe" of every hexagon in a city from Overture places. Isaac closes with roughly 100 TB of ERA5 and gridMET climate data in an H3-native format on Source Cooperative.
- **[The Decision Satellites Can't See](/blog/the-decision-satellites-cant-see)** by Kristin Scholten (Nationwide). Predicting crop acreage for the 2026 season with H3, and when to convert from H3 back to field boundaries.
- **[H3 as a GeoParquet Pyramid Builder](/blog/h3-geoparquet-pyramid-builder)** by Guillaume Sueur (Geomermaids). Using H3 cells to build level-of-detail pyramids for GeoParquet, the way overviews work for Cloud Optimized GeoTIFFs.
- **[Lessons from Mapping Earth Observation Data with H3](/blog/mapping-earth-observation-data-with-h3)** by Marie Hoeger (Carbon Mapper). Combining multi-resolution layers on H3 to score tree planting sites in Brazil, computed on request in Fused, and mapping sparse methane plume detections.
- **[The Geography of Basketball](/blog/geography-of-basketball)** by Matt Forrest (Wherobots). An H3 index of basketball built from about 91,000 outdoor courts, indoor courts, team territories and player hometowns.
- **[Is Your GeoAI Solving for Earth or for Flat Maps?](/blog/geoai-for-earth-or-flat-maps)** by Levente Juhász (University of Florida). How projection distortion leaks into pixel-based models, and how H3 compares with S2, A5, ISEA and HEALPix grids.
- **[H3 in the Field](/blog/h3-in-the-field)** by Mo Fotouhi (John Deere). Extending H3 for field-level, time-aware agricultural analysis without changing its core.
- **[Indexing Energy-Intensive Infrastructure with H3](/blog/indexing-energy-infrastructure-with-h3)** by Robert Murphy (Othersphere). Indexing global infrastructure data on H3 for site screening and asset financial modeling.
- **[One Grid for Outdoors and Indoors](/blog/one-grid-for-outdoors-and-indoors)** by Sean Gorman (Zephr). Organizing GNSS corrections and indoor visual map tiles on two resolutions of the same H3 index.
- **[The Foursquare H3 Hub](/blog/foursquare-h3-hub)** by Xun Li (Foursquare). An open Apache Iceberg catalog of 103 H3-indexed datasets, a desktop app and a spatial agent that joins layers on shared cells.
- **[Maps from the Newsroom to the Pen Plotter](/blog/maps-from-newsroom-to-pen-plotter)** by Tim Wallace (The New York Times). A tour of maps made for the news and for fun.
